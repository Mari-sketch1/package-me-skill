# Package Me v4.2 evals

## Eval 1 — rich multifaceted dossier

Input: духовная практика, профессиональные роли, творчество, проекты, отношения и живой voice.

Expected:

- facet map before positioning;
- person-first, portfolio and at least one other architecture considered;
- no single life story inflated into the whole brand;
- stop at architecture gate.

## Eval 2 — user says «это только одна моя грань»

Expected:

- acknowledge architectural error without defending copy;
- return to facet map;
- replace narrow territories with person-first/portfolio/hybrid options;
- record anti-reduction constraint.

Failure: broaden the same niche with more adjacent topics.

## Eval 3 — user wants a narrow expert brand

Expected:

- territory-led architecture remains available;
- preserve supporting facets without forcing equal weight;
- make the user's explicit goal authoritative.

## Eval 4 — portfolio becomes «blog about everything»

Expected:

- strengthen connective spine, memory anchors and audience entry doors;
- do not delete facets merely for neatness.

## Eval 5 — user chooses a hybrid

Expected:

- base architecture + connective spine + current emphasis;
- no A+B+C role list in one bio.

## Eval 6 — accurate but dull copy

Expected:

- Copy Lab rejects generic lines;
- first screen works as composition rather than biography dump;
- reduction test protects other facets.

## Eval 7 — Fast mode

Expected:

- internally compare architectures and routes;
- choose with rationale;
- keep up to three strong alternatives.

## Eval 8 — source and tradition integrity

Expected:

- lived meaning can lead;
- source remains visible according to dossier;
- no false authority or medical promise.

## Eval 9 — final delivery

Expected:

- `PACKAGING_DECISIONS.md` and `SOCIAL_PACKAGING.md`;
- facet constellation and connective spine are explicit;
- platform copy is vivid, usable and does not imprison future content.

## Eval 10 — four nearly identical names and dull bios

Input: несколько названий и bio повторяют одну мысль с переставленными словами; пользователь говорит «все одинаковое» и «скучно».

Expected:

- acknowledge a divergence failure, not defend individual wording;
- discard semantic duplicates;
- independently generate 20–30 rough names across at least five directions and shortlist 5–7 across at least four;
- independently generate 15–20 rough bios across at least five rhetorical structures and shortlist 5–7;
- present names and bios as separate numbered lists with approach, effect, strength and risk;
- combine only after the user selects directions.

Failure: polish the same premise with synonyms or return fixed `Name 1 + Bio 1` packages.

## Eval 11 — variation beyond copy

Input: user asks for options for hooks, CTA, content structure and visual direction.

Expected:

- each set uses genuinely different mechanisms;
- visual options are different concepts, not palette variations;
- CTA options imply different actions or commitment levels;
- content architectures organize material differently rather than renaming the same pillars.

## Eval 12 — strongest name and strongest bio do not share an index

Expected:

- components remain independently selectable;
- no pressure to choose a pre-bundled option;
- after selection, produce 2–4 compatible combinations and explain trade-offs.

## Eval 13 — every turn advances the user

Input: пользователь выбрал или самостоятельно сформулировал название и bio.

Expected:

- explicitly mark the combination as selected, needs revision, or still exploratory;
- do not end with praise or an optional offer;
- move to the nearest unfinished stage;
- end with one concrete `Следующий шаг` that can be answered in one short reply.

Failure: end with «можем продолжить», ask «что думаете?» or offer several unrelated future actions.

## Eval 14 — accurate identity, weak cold entry

Input: title и bio точно описывают автора, его духовную роль и традицию, но новый человек не знает терминов и не понимает, зачем оставаться.

Expected:

- preserve the person-first internal architecture;
- redesign the first screen around a clear question, interest or promise of useful understanding;
- build a disclosure ladder: cold entry → value proof → author identity → worldview/source → deep layer;
- evaluate unfamiliar terms by their immediate explanatory value and move them later when appropriate without concealing the source;
- run the 5–10 second first-impression test on the final title + description/bio combination.

Failure: either keep an identity-correct but audience-incomprehensible first screen, or erase the author's worldview and tradition entirely.
